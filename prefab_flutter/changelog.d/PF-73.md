- **Breaking:** annotations follow the vocabulary of decision-2. Hard renames, without deprecated aliases (PF-73):
  - `@View(...)` → `@Entity(...)`
  - `ActionPlace.detail` / `.list` / `.item` → `Place.detail` / `.list` / `.item`
  - `@Field(views: {'list', 'detail'})` → `@Field(on: {Place.item, Place.detail, Place.form})`; `on` also controls the form, and a field without `on` appears everywhere
  - `@Field(hidden: true)` → `@Field(on: {})`
  - `FormOptions(custom: ...)` → `FormOptions(builder: ...)`
  - `@Field(display: ...)` → `@Field(displayBuilder: ...)`
  - `Parent.type` → `Parent.entity`
  - the deprecated `@FormField` is removed; use `@Field` (PF-73)
